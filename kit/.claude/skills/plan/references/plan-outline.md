## Timeline rules (use these, label them)
| Step | Typical time | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Setup (repo, database or local store, keys, first free run) | 1 day of the owner's clicks | assumed |
| New sending domains + mailboxes, warm-up before cold sends | 2–4 weeks; pre-warmed mailboxes skip most of it | sourced (generic playbook) |
| Daily volume per mailbox | start 10–15, at most 20–30 including follow-ups; 2–3 mailboxes per domain | sourced + measured |
| First judgement on a channel or copy variant | ≥ 30 delivered and after follow-up 1 went out | measured rule |
| Targeting or offer check | reply rate under about 1% after 300+ sends → change targeting or offer, don't send more | sourced (vendor threshold) |
| Partner channel | first meetings take weeks; judge at 30 and 90 days after signing | assumed |

How to phrase results: "By week N you will have: M companies screened, K qualified, about J verified contacts a
day (the author's daily runs gave about 25 queued, measured 2026-11-05/06)". Replies: "we don't forecast replies;
the author's own cold sends had 0 human replies from 119 first emails to 2026-11-06, which is why the plan has
stop-loss rules". Never a percentage that is not measured.
