# Channel picker

Score each channel 0 to 2 on each question for this company, then pick 2 to 4 with the highest totals. Ties: the
cheaper one first. Every pick needs one sentence of evidence from the research.

1. **Signal:** is there a public, dated sign that a buyer needs this now? (tool fingerprint, register entry, job
   post, review theme, post they commented on)
2. **Fact:** can we name one checkable fact about *their* system or business in the first message?
3. **Reach:** can we get a named person's verified email, or meet them where they already talk?
4. **Cost:** can it start on free tiers and stay under the default $30 a month of paid data?
5. **Fit with the offer:** price and sales cycle (a $50k offer can afford slow partner work; a $500 one can't).

|---|---|---|---|---|

Details, pitfalls and when each fits: [non-obvious-channels.md](non-obvious-channels.md),

## Rules for the pick
- One channel is "first": the one with a signal and a fact. Others start after the first has ≥ 30 delivered.
- A channel the buyer already gets spammed through (generic cold email to a crowded segment) scores 0 on Fact
  unless we have a measured fact.
- Comments and communities are research sources first, lead sources second.
- Say what we are not doing and why.
