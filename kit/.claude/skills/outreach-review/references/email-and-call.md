# Email plus call, LinkedIn by hand, SMS (generic practice; not legal advice)

## Calls after email
Open and click tracking stay off (deliverability; Apple Mail makes opens meaningless), so calls follow *replies* and
the *end of the sequence*, not opens:
- "not now" or a question → call on the date they gave, or the same day.
- Positive → same-day call (or the booking link at once).
- Finished all steps with no reply and a phone number → one call a few days after the last email.
- Cold numbers: a few attempts, then park in nurture and move on (generic; set your own limit from your data).
- Present on the first real conversation; keep following up a presented lead until a decision.
Before any cold call: check the number, screen it against the national do-not-call lists (UK: TPS for individuals and
sole traders, CTPS for companies), use a valid local caller ID, and say the call may be recorded.

## LinkedIn by hand
No scraping and no automation on the owner's profile (LinkedIn's terms ban both). Under about 100 invitations a week,
withdraw invitations pending more than 3 weeks, a short note or none after a profile view and a comment. Commenters
from source-social go here first; email only when a verified address exists. Vendor benchmarks (Expandi, sourced):
28.5% accept, 10.4% reply after accept; treat as an upper bound.

## SMS
Only for known contacts (after a missed call, a reminder), never cold. In the UK, texts to limited companies need no
prior consent but must name the sender and offer an opt-out; sole traders need consent, and some lawyers read the
corporate exemption as not covering SMS at all (sourced, conflicting): check before use.
