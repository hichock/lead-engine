# Social sources: compliance notes (not legal advice)

- **LinkedIn:** the User Agreement bans scraping and automation tools. Never use the owner's logged-in session or
  cookies for scraping. No connect or DM automation on the owner's profile: manual connects, about 100 a week.
  Cookie-free actors keep the owner's account out of it but don't make the use compliant. LinkedIn won against hiQ
  (2022), and Proxycurl shut down after LinkedIn sued it (2025) (sourced in the author's KB).
- Store minimal fields (name, headline, company, post topic), never resell, never publish lists.
- **GDPR (UK/EU):** names and headlines are personal data. Keep a legitimate-interest note, tell people where you
  found them at first contact, honour objections at once.
