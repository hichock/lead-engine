# Commenters: classify, contact, cost

## Classes (rules)
- **buyer** = buyer role + target company type, blocked by seller words.
- **seller** = freelancer, automation agency, creator, VA, student, empty bio.
- **peer/adjacent** = the rest.
On LinkedIn 46% of commenters were non-ICP and 50% adjacent, mostly peers who sell (measured 2026-10-29).
Rule check: 26–27 of 30 agreed with hand labels.

## Contact (measured 2026-10-29)
- Domain found for 9 of 15 buyers; a personal `ok` address for 2 (13%); any address for 3 (20%).
- Each domain took 2–3 minutes of searching. The money isn't the bottleneck: the search time per person is.
- Cost ≈ $0.16 per buyer and ≈ $1.07 per verified email.
→ Commenters go to manual LinkedIn connects first (connect without a note, message 2 days after accept), and to email
only when a verified address exists.

## LinkedIn manual limits
About 100 connection requests a week. Vendor benchmarks: 28.5% accept, 10.4% reply after accept (Expandi, sourced,
vendor data: treat as an upper bound).
