# Comments research (once per prospect)

Source: the bundle author's runs and decisions, 2026-11-06.

- Actors: `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-comments` and `harvestapi/linkedin-profile-reactions`, $2/1k each
  (sourced 2026-11-08). 11 prospects → 342 comments + 440 likes ≈ $1.60 (measured 2026-11-06), about $0.15 each.
- Claude summarises topics, creators and tools they engage with, then picks the offer from **all** activity.
- Result: for 9 of 11 prospects, a different offer fit better than the post where they were found (measured).
- The same data ranks creators by how many buyers engage with them, which feeds the source list (source-social).
- The opener names the interest, never "I saw your comment".
- No cookie, no login: the owner's LinkedIn account stays out of it. That keeps the account safe; it does not make
  scraping compliant with LinkedIn's terms (see source-social/references/compliance.md).
