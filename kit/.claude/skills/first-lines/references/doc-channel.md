# Optional channel: a shared Google Doc (author's test, 2026-11-08; results: no figure yet)

How it worked: a script inside a separate Google Workspace mailbox creates a Google Doc (the free check written up)
and shares it with the prospect with notify on and a 2-line note. It lands in Gmail's Updates tab and shows only the
title and the note, so **the title is the hook** (a measured fact, e.g. "acme portal: 5.4 s on a phone").

Rules the author set:
- 5 shares a day in week 1, then 10, at most ~20 per account.
- Follow-ups by email on days 3 and 8 ("I shared a short doc on <day> titled …").
- Unshare after 14 days without a reply.
- Stop on a quota error, a Google warning, or 2 angry replies in 50.
- One public backlash case exists (Feb 2026, sourced): treat this as a risky channel and keep volume low.
- Never from the main brand account.

This bundle does not automate the doc channel. If the owner wants it, write the doc titles and notes here as drafts
and keep the counts in `kb/runs/`.
